Associate Software Engineer (Level 10) 
Scope: 
✅
 Task 
Summary: A new engineer at the start of their career, focussed on expanding their knowledge and skill-set to further 
increase their impact within a team. 
“Associate Software Engineers” are at the first stage of their software engineering career. They’re contributing to the team’s goals 
through executing on well defined tasks, with support from more experienced engineers.

Engineer I (Level 20) 
Scope: 
✅
 Task 
Summary: An engineer with high potential, developing the fundamentals they need to be successful in their team. 
“Engineer I” engineers are in the earlier stages of their software engineering career. They’re working on well-defined tasks and are 
supported by the team when stuck. They’re expected to ask lots of questions! As someone progresses toward “Engineer II” they 
should start to gain confidence to pick up larger or less well-defined tasks with less required support. 
“Engineer I” engineers are expected to progress to “Engineer II” within one and a half years at Monzo. 

Writes well tested code and documentation in-line with our Engineering Principles. 
Able to safely release code to production in collaboration with more experienced engineers. 
Demonstrates understanding of Monzo’s system architecture. 
Debugs and resolves issues related to their code changes in production.

Proactively learns from the work of others through activities such as reading & commenting on pull requests and proposals their team produces. 
Proactively communicates to their team what they are working on, why, how it's going and what help they need. 
Asks questions to understand how to write effective code, and prioritises their own learning to better serve their team. 
Proactively improves the test coverage and documentation of existing code.

Engineer II (Level 30) 
Scope: 
✅
 Task 
Summary: A productive and reliable engineer working effectively within a team 
This level defines engineers who have learned the ropes, and are building the foundation to being solid Individual Contributors at 
Monzo. Engineer II engineers are focussed on tasks assigned to them within their team, typically working with the support of other 
engineers to reliably deliver these*. 
Engineer II engineers are expected to progress to Engineer III within 2 years at Monzo.

Delivers tasks and designs effective solutions to product features or small scope engineering obstacles in their area of the 
business. 
Makes steps towards owning the end-to-end development of a feature, or problem space, including the ability to identify, define, 
and scope problems. 
Breaks down medium sized engineering problems in collaboration with more senior engineers. 
Demonstrates ownership of smaller problem spaces, and repeatedly achieving successful outcomes for those problems.

Writes clear, well separated code that reflects coding guidelines and conventions, and can be easily understood by others. 
Proactively tests their work appropriately. Provides clear and actionable technical feedback on pull requests. 
Aligns with our Engineering Principles when giving feedback on other’s code.  
Takes responsibility for the quality of their work. Owns and actions fixing defects. 
Demonstrating awareness of key non-functional principles in their discipline/domain (accessibility, performance, security, etc) and evidence of actioning them.

Gives direct feedback to others in their team, including giving useful and actionable feedback after reviewing their work. 
Accepts feedback from others graciously. Makes consistent, steady progress on work assigned to them.  
Proactively communicates progress to their team, attempts to unblock themselves, but seeks timely guidance where needed. 
Proactively documents existing and new features or projects Makes consistent contributions as a floater/release owner
